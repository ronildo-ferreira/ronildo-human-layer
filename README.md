# Ronildo Ferreira

Human Layer of the Zaion Ecosystem.

This account represents the human perspective behind the educational and technological projects connected to the Zaion ecosystem.

Here I reflect on:

• technology and society  
• artificial intelligence and education  
• digital identity  
• the relationship between human thought and technological systems  

Other parts of the ecosystem:

• Zahroniel — educational projects  
• Zaion — web development  
• Kael Élodie Whitmore — artificial intelligence  

Technology is not only about machines and code.
It is about people learning how to live in a changing world.

Technology evolves.
Systems grow.
Artificial intelligence expands.

But every system still depends on one essential element:

The human layer.
